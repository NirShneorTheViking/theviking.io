From fjord level back into the mountains

The last chapter is almost as long as the first: 144.18 km. Its elevation profile begins virtually at sea level and repeatedly climbs back above 1,000 metres, giving the finish a different character from the high central plateau.

The photographs show softer late-summer light, open moorland, lakes, cloudberries and the long northern views of the final days. The archived route runs through the Fjordruta area before turning inland toward Oppdal — the end of a 50-day, 550-kilometre crossing.

This chapter is reconstructed only from material that survives on the original site. I have kept the narrative deliberately factual rather than inventing day-by-day memories that were never published there.
