The journey

Where the tarmac ends, the real route begins.

In November 2007, right after finishing five years in the military, I set out across New Zealand for three months. The rhythm was simple: drive the unsealed gravel roads until the track narrowed to a footpath, shoulder a pack, and disappear into the mountains and coastal bush for days at a time.
