A week in the wild

Jotunheimen — the Home of the Giants. A vast landscape of rugged peaks, deep valleys, lakes and endless mountain terrain. This was not just another hike, but a journey into the heart of Norway's wild mountain country.

Covering more than 53 kilometres with over 1,600 metres of cumulative climbing, the crossing demanded endurance, determination and the ability to keep moving day after day. The landscape changed constantly, but the mountains remained the defining feature of the journey.

A week away from the ordinary — surrounded by wilderness, carrying what we needed, and moving through one of Norway's most impressive mountain regions.

53 kilometres. A week in the wild. A challenging mountain crossing through Jotunheimen, with long distances, significant climbing and unforgettable Norwegian scenery.
