The long and difficult way to Danskehytta

At 36.29 kilometres and more than 1,800 metres of cumulative climbing, this was one of the toughest days of the journey. A long mountain trek that demanded endurance, determination and a lot more energy than we expected.

The Norwegian mountains kept challenging us throughout the day. Every kilometre brought another climb, another stretch of demanding terrain and another reason to keep pushing forward. Reaching Danskehytta was more than just arriving at our destination — it was the reward for a seriously hard day out.

36 kilometres. One very long day. A demanding mountain journey with significant elevation gain, challenging terrain and a memorable finish at Danskehytta.
