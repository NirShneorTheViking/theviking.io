The journey

Twenty-one days. Five challenges.

Two years after my first crossing, I returned to Norway with friends. This time, the plan was different: leave the huts behind, carry a tent, and take on five challenging routes — from the fjords and Jotunheimen to the dramatic landscapes around Lysefjord.
