Granite slabs and sheer drops

Starting from Øygardstøl at the upper reach of Lysevegen, the route wastes no time—launching straight into steep, smooth granite slabs and chain-assisted climbs across three distinct mountain ridges. Every step up rewards you with expanding views over the rugged high plateau and down toward Lysebotn.

Across the bare rock plateau, the path leads out toward Kjerag and Kjeragbolten—the famous boulder wedged tightly inside a narrow rock crevice suspended roughly a thousand metres above Lysefjorden. The sheer vertical drop on either side makes the exposure unmistakable.

Working back across the undulating granite slabs and descending the steep rock faces brings the hike full circle to Lysevegen, high above the fjord in raw alpine terrain.

Three ridges above the fjord. A steep, exposed granite hike from Øygardstøl and Lysevegen out to Kjerag and Kjeragbolten.
