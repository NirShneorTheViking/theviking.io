From the fjord to the summit

The climb to Skåla is a journey from the shores of Nordfjord to one of Norway's most spectacular mountain summits, rising to 1,843 metres above sea level.

Starting near Loen, the trail climbs relentlessly through forest, open mountainside and increasingly rugged terrain. With the fjord gradually disappearing below, the final ascent reveals the iconic Skålatårnet standing alone above the surrounding peaks.

Loen to Skåla A demanding mountain ascent, gaining almost 1,800 metres from fjord level to the summit.
