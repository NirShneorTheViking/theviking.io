High above the fjord

Leaving the bustling trail toward Preikestolen at Neverdalsskardet, the route turns northeast into quieter, wilder terrain along the northern wall of Lysefjorden. Almost immediately, the crowds vanish, replaced by dense mixed woodland, mountain tarns, and rushing cascades cutting through Neverdalen.

Climbing out onto the exposed rock ridges beneath Bratteliknuten and past the sheer drop of Hengjanenibba, the fjord opens up far below. Walking the cliff edge offers sweeping views both deep into Lysefjorden and back toward the vertical walls that define this part of Rogaland.

The final stretch drops steeply through birch and ash forest down to Bratteli—an isolated, sun-drenched mountain farm clinging to the slope above the water, accessible only by foot or by fjord boat.

Cliffs, forest, and sheer fjord drops. A rugged point-to-point traverse along the northern edge of Lysefjorden, ending at a secluded quay reachable only by boat.
