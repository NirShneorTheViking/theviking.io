Into the high mountains

Shorter in distance than the long stretches around it, this stage pushes higher than any other in the archive—topping out at 2,049 metres on the western edge of Jotunheimen as the route threads north toward Sognefjell.

The terrain shifts rapidly along the way: lush green valleys and red DNT trail markers giving way to snowfields, steep rock, and exposed high ground where the air turns crisp and thin.

Every climb sharpens the contrast that defines this corridor of Norway—sheltered valleys and mountain huts tucked far below, and unbroken snow, glaciers, and wide alpine horizons stretching out above.
