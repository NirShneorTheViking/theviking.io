Hardangervidda and the long opening stretch

Leaving Haukeliseter behind, the journey opens with its longest and most expansive stage: nearly 192 kilometres heading north across the vast, windswept plateau of Hardangervidda toward Breistølen.

This is where the scale of the crossing sets in. Day after day, the trail weaves between glacial lakes, open tundra, and broad stony passes—repeatedly climbing above 1,500 metres where the horizon stretches endlessly in every direction.

With long distances between mountain huts and weather that shifts without warning, the opening leg tests patience as much as legs—settling into the steady, self-reliant rhythm that carries the rest of the expedition north.
