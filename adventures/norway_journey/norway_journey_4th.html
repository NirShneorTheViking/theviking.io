A loop through high valleys

Covering 75.13 kilometres from Grotli through Pyttbua to Zakanasvatnet, this fifth stage brings a wider rhythm of terrain: looping around Breiddalsvatnet before pushing deep into the wild, open plateaus of Reinheimen and turning north-west.

High mountain lakes and windswept stony ridges give way to long, sweeping descents into lush green valleys—dropping all the way to a low point around 403 metres and packing relentless vertical movement into every day on the trail.

Lower down along the valley floors, late-summer walking slows the pace in the best way—pausing along the path to pick wild mountain berries before the final push toward the glacial waters of Zakanasvatnet.
