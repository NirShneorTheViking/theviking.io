Snow, water and summer colour

At 63.59 kilometres, this is the shortest chapter of the crossing, threading north from Bøvertun into the wild, glacier-carved mountains of Breheimen toward Sota.

The terrain rarely flattens for long. The trail rises and dips across wet mountain passes, repeatedly climbing back above 1,400 metres where lingering snowfields feed cold alpine lakes and rushing meltwater streams.

Lower down along the valley slopes, bright pink fireweed bursts through the rock and moss—capturing that brief Norwegian mountain window where high summer and winter share the very same frame.
