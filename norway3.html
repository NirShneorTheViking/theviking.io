Into the high mountains

The third week is shorter in distance but reaches the highest point shown anywhere in the archived elevation profiles: 2,049 metres. The photographs shift between green valleys, snow, exposed high ground and DNT trail markers — a compact chapter with a distinctly alpine feel.

The route map places this stage on the western edge of Jotunheimen, threading north toward Sognefjell. The old collage captures the contrast that defines this part of Norway: low valleys and huts below, snowfields and broad mountain views above.

This chapter is reconstructed only from material that survives on the original site. I have kept the narrative deliberately factual rather than inventing day-by-day memories that were never published there.
