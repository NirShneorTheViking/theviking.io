Hardangervidda and the long opening stretch

The opening leg is the longest chapter in the archive: nearly 192 km north from Haukeliseter. The original collage shows a landscape still carrying large fields of snow, frozen water, reindeer on the plateau and the first long rhythm of hut-to-hut travel.

This is where the crossing becomes real: a route line stretching north across the central mountains, with the elevation profile repeatedly climbing above 1,500 metres. The visual archive is deliberately kept intact here — map, profile and trail photographs together — because that was the language of the original site.

This chapter is reconstructed only from material that survives on the original site. I have kept the narrative deliberately factual rather than inventing day-by-day memories that were never published there.
