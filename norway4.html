Snow, water and summer colour

At 63.59 km, this is the shortest chapter of the crossing. The archive shows a landscape of lingering snow, lakes, wet mountain passes and bright fireweed — summer and winter sharing the same frame.

The elevation profile is constantly rolling, with repeated climbs back above 1,400 metres. The map and photographs place the route among the mountains around Breheimen, continuing the northward progression toward Sota.

This chapter is reconstructed only from material that survives on the original site. I have kept the narrative deliberately factual rather than inventing day-by-day memories that were never published there.
